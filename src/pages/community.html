We want to hear from you: what you run, what breaks, and which machines you want Nerv on next.

Chat

Join the official Nerv channel on Telegram to follow what we are building and talk to the people building it. For longer questions, setups worth showing off and discussions that should stay findable, post on Reddit in r/nervlinux. Releases and news go out on X as @nervlinux.

Join on Telegram r/nervlinux on Reddit @nervlinux on X

Contributors work together on our own Matrix server, at #nerv:nervlinux.org. To get access, message @r3tex on Telegram.

Contribute

Nerv is open source under Apache-2.0, and contributors are welcome: code, packaging, documentation, testing on your hardware, or a good bug report. The source is on Codeberg. Say hello in the chat, or write to hello@nervlinux.org.

Contributions come in under Apache-2.0, the licence they go out under, signed off with the Developer Certificate of Origin (git commit -s). There is no contributor agreement to sign; CONTRIBUTING.md has the rest.
