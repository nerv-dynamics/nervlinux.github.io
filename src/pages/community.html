Community

We want to hear from you: what you run, what breaks, and which machines you want Nerv on next.

Chat

Join the official Nerv channel on Telegram to follow what we are building and talk to the people building it.

Join on Telegram

Contributors work together on our own Matrix server, at #nerv:nervlinux.org, bridged to IRC. To get access, message @r3tex on Telegram.

Contribute

Nerv is open source under Apache-2.0, and contributors are welcome: code, packaging, documentation, testing on your hardware, or a good bug report. Our GitLab opens to the public soon. Until then, say hello in the chat or write to hello@nervlinux.org.

Security

Report vulnerabilities privately to security@nervlinux.org, not in the chat.
